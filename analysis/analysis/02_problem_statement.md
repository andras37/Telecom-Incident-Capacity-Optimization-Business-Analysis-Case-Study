# Problem Statement

## Business Context
The telecommunications company operates a large-scale customer support organization responsible for handling technical, billing, and general service incidents.

Over time, the volume of incoming incidents has increased while service expectations have remained high. Multiple support teams handle incidents with different skill profiles, capacities, and responsibilities, all governed by defined Service Level Agreements (SLAs).

---

## Observed Problem
Despite having operational data available, the organization experiences:
- Increasing SLA breaches  
- Uneven workload distribution across support teams  
- Significant variance in incident resolution times  
- Limited transparency into which incident types and teams drive operational risk  

Operational decisions are often reactive, based on recent escalations rather than structured analysis.

---

## Why This Is a Business Problem
SLA breaches and prolonged resolution times directly impact:
- Customer satisfaction and retention risk  
- Operational costs due to rework, escalations, and overtime  
- Management confidence in prioritization and investment decisions  

Without a clear understanding of where the true bottlenecks lie, improvement initiatives risk addressing symptoms rather than root causes.

---

## Stakeholders Affected
- **Operations Management** – accountable for team performance and capacity  
- **Service Delivery / SLA Owners** – responsible for contractual service levels  
- **Finance** – concerned with cost efficiency and return on improvement efforts  
- **Customer Experience** – focused on satisfaction and churn risk  

Each stakeholder group views the problem from a different perspective, making prioritization decisions complex.

---

## Core Decision Challenge
The key business challenge is to determine:
- Which incident categories and teams create the highest operational impact  
- Where limited resources should be allocated to achieve the greatest improvement in SLA compliance  
- Which potential improvement actions can be deprioritized without significant business risk  

This project aims to support these decisions by providing a structured, data-informed analysis rather than isolated metrics or dashboards.
