# Executive Summary

## Context
The telecommunications company is facing increasing pressure on its customer support operations due to growing incident volumes, uneven team workloads, and rising SLA breaches.

While operational data is available, management currently lacks a clear, decision-oriented view of where interventions would deliver the highest business impact under limited capacity and budget constraints.

---

## Key Findings
The analysis highlights three core insights:

1. **Operational impact is highly concentrated**  
A small subset of incident categories and teams drives a disproportionate share of SLA breaches, long resolution times, and repeat incidents.

2. **Capacity is not the primary constraint**  
Overall support capacity exists, but workload distribution and incident handling processes create bottlenecks that reduce effective throughput.

3. **Not all improvement actions deliver equal value**  
Broad capacity expansion provides short-term relief but comes with high recurring costs and limited long-term efficiency gains.

---

## Decision Options Considered
Three strategic options were evaluated:

- **Capacity Reallocation and Workload Balancing**  
- **Targeted Process Improvement for High-Impact Incidents**  
- **Capacity Expansion**

Each option was assessed based on expected business impact, cost efficiency, implementation risk, and sustainability.

---

## Recommended Direction
The recommended approach is to prioritize **Targeted Process Improvement for High-Impact Incidents**, supported by selective workload rebalancing where necessary.

This direction offers the strongest balance between:
- SLA compliance improvement  
- Operational efficiency gains  
- Cost control  

Capacity expansion should be treated as a secondary option and considered only if demand growth continues after process improvements have been implemented.

---

## Expected Business Impact
If implemented successfully, the recommended approach is expected to:
- Reduce SLA breaches in the most critical incident categories  
- Shorten resolution times for high-impact incidents  
- Lower repeat incident rates  
- Improve customer experience while controlling operational costs  

---

## BA Role and Value
The Business Analyst’s role in this initiative is to:
- Translate operational data into decision-relevant insights  
- Make trade-offs and assumptions explicit  
- Support management with evidence-based prioritization  

This enables proactive, structured decision-making rather than reactive escalation-driven responses.
## Decision Required

Approve targeted capacity rebalancing and focused process interventions for high-volume teams in order to reduce SLA breach exposure and stabilize operational performance within the next quarter.
