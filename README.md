# Telecom Incident Capacity Optimization  
### Business Analysis Case Study (Power BI)

## Executive Summary

This project analyzes operational incident data from a telecommunications provider to identify the primary drivers of SLA breaches and prolonged resolution times.

By combining incident volume, resolution duration, and SLA compliance metrics, the analysis demonstrates that SLA underperformance is primarily driven by **structural capacity constraints** rather than isolated team inefficiencies.

The outcome supports **data-driven capacity reallocation and root-cause prioritization**, enabling management to reduce SLA breach rates, improve customer experience, and stabilize operational performance under high demand.

**Key result:** actionable prioritization of intervention areas with clear operational impact.

---

## Business Context

The telecom provider experiences recurring SLA breaches despite stable team structures and defined operational processes. Management lacks clarity on whether SLA issues stem from:
- insufficient capacity,
- inefficient execution,
- or uneven incident distribution across teams.

This ambiguity limits effective decision-making and leads to reactive rather than preventive interventions.

---

## Business Questions Addressed

- Where do SLA breaches concentrate across teams and incident categories?
- Is SLA non-compliance driven by incident volume, resolution complexity, or execution inefficiency?
- Which teams should be prioritized for capacity intervention?
- What operational actions would most effectively reduce SLA breach exposure?

---

## Analytical Approach

The project follows a structured **Business Analysis** workflow:

1. **Problem structuring**  
   Framing the operational issue and decision context

2. **Stakeholder mapping**  
   Identifying decision-makers and impacted roles

3. **KPI tree development**  
   Linking operational metrics to business outcomes

4. **Exploratory and diagnostic analysis**  
   Identifying patterns in volume, resolution time, and SLA breaches

5. **Decision option evaluation**  
   Comparing alternative intervention strategies

---

## Key Insights

- Teams handling higher incident volumes consistently show longer resolution times.
- SLA breaches concentrate where workload exceeds effective capacity.
- Performance issues indicate **systemic capacity imbalance**, not individual team underperformance.
- Targeted capacity rebalancing yields higher expected impact than uniform process tightening.

---

## Decision Options Evaluated

- **Option A:** Maintain current structure and focus on efficiency improvements  
- **Option B:** Targeted staffing and workload reallocation for high-volume teams  
- **Option C (Recommended):** Capacity rebalancing combined with root-cause reduction initiatives  

Option C provides the strongest balance between implementation effort and SLA improvement potential.

---

## Dashboard Overview (Power BI)

The Power BI dashboard provides executive-level visibility into:

- Total incident volume by team
- Average resolution time
- SLA breach rate
- Cross-team capacity pressure visualization
- Executive explanation page linking metrics to decisions

Visuals are designed for **storytelling**, not exploration.

The Power BI dashboard directly supports executive decision-making by visualizing capacity-driven SLA risk and linking analytical insights to concrete intervention options.
